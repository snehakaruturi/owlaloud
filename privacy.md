# OwlAloud Privacy Policy and Terms

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Publisher: Sneha Karuturi

Contact: snehakaruturi9@gmail.com

## 1. Summary

OwlAloud reads text you select aloud. It does not collect, store or share your personal information.

## 2. Text you read

OwlAloud copies the text you select, speaks it using the voices installed in Windows, and then restores your previous clipboard content. The text is processed on your PC only. It is not stored and it is not sent anywhere. If you turn on the setting "If nothing is selected, read the latest clipboard content", OwlAloud reads the text on your clipboard in the same way. Text is spoken out loud, so please be mindful when reading private text in shared spaces.

## 3. Information stored on your device

OwlAloud stores a settings file (voice, speed, volume, shortcut key and options) and a small log file with times, errors and character counts. The log does not contain the text you read. These files stay on your device.

## 4. What OwlAloud does not do

OwlAloud has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. OwlAloud itself does not connect to the internet and does not share data with anyone.

## 5. Third parties

OwlAloud speaks using voices installed in Windows, which are provided by Microsoft and run on your device. If you get OwlAloud from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and updates and any data it collects under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. OwlAloud does not receive that data.

## 6. Children

OwlAloud is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Your rights

Because OwlAloud does not collect or hold your personal data, the publisher holds no personal data about you to access, correct or delete. Your settings and log files stay on your device and you can delete them at any time. If you have questions, contact the email above.

## 8. Security

All processing happens on your device. The text you select is only as safe as your device and the apps you take it from, so please keep your device secure.

## 9. Start with Windows

If enabled, OwlAloud adds itself to your startup apps. You can turn this off in OwlAloud Settings or in Windows Settings.

## 10. Removing OwlAloud

Uninstalling OwlAloud removes the app. You can delete any remaining settings and log files from your user profile.
